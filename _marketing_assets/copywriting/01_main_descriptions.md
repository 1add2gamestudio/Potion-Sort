## 版本 A：硬核 / 玩法策略向（突出核心机制、操作手感与策略深度）

### 中文版
**不止是倒水，是一场精密编排的炼金博弈。**

《日光炼金瓶（Solstice Sort）》是一款围绕“物理倾倒手感”与“高策略解谜节奏”打造的竖屏分色解谜游戏。所有主线关卡均经过内置求解器逐关校验，确保 **100% 无需道具即可纯脑力通关**，拒绝任何无解死局。

每关顶部配备优雅的**限时速通倒计时**，在目标时间内以最少步数封瓶即可斩获额外速通积分与金币；配合 3 阶**连胜祝福**、**日光炼金台精华蒸馏**与内置的**无障碍色盲符号模式**，无论你是速通大师还是逻辑解谜爱好者，都能在指尖清脆的玻璃碰撞与咕嘟倒液声中，享受酣畅淋漓的破局快感。

**More than pouring colors — a precision alchemy puzzle engineered for real strategy.**

*Solstice Sort* reimagines liquid-sorting puzzles with tactile pouring physics and deeply structured board logic. Every campaign level is validated by our deterministic solver so every single board is **100% solvable without boosters** — zero rigged dead-ends.

Progress along our signature **Golden Wave Sawtooth** difficulty curve: start with a crisp 2-color warm-up, dive straight into a 5-color tactical spike on Level 2, and test your planning on 7-color Super Hard Boss boards. Four escalating obstacle mechanics transform simple sorting into multi-step sequencing: hidden **Mystery "?" Beat each level's subtle **Speed Bonus countdown** for extra points and coins, build a **Solstice Win Streak** for starting perks, distill collected essences into boosters at the **Sun Alembic**, and tackle mechanic-focused **Azure Gala Trials** anytime.
