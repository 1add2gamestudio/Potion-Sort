Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to play?
No. The game is fully offline — no sign-up, no login, no internet required.
Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?
Progress is stored on the device only. Deleting the app erases it. There is no cloud save in this version.
Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?
No. This version contains no advertising of any kind (not even simulated ad screens) and no in-app purchases. Every booster is bought with in-game coins or crafted from essences earned by clearing levels.
Is there a time limit on levels?
No. There is no countdown timer and no move limit on any level — you can take as long as you need. Levels cannot be failed by running out of time.
How do Win Streaks work?
Clear consecutive levels to build a streak. At streak 1 your opening move pours 1 step automatically, at streak 2 it pours 2 steps, and from streak 3 onward you also get one free Undo.
Can I interact with the fox and the dragon?
Yes. Tap either mascot on the shelf and they answer with their own line in a cloud-shaped speech bubble. It fades on its own after a few seconds.
A level feels impossible. Am I stuck?
Every level is validated by our solver and is 100% solvable without boosters. Use the undo and shuffle boosters freely — they are there for exactly this.
No sound on my iPhone?
Check the silent switch on the side of the device and the volume buttons. Then open in-game Settings and confirm both Sound and Music are on.
How do I change the language?
Open in-game Settings and select English or 简体中文. The game follows the system language on first launch.
Can I play without colors?
Yes. Turn on Color Symbols in Settings to show a unique symbol on each potion color — useful for color-vision deficiency.

中文常见问题

  • 需要账号吗？不需要，纯单机离线，无登录。
  • 卸载会丢档吗？会。进度仅存本地，当前版本没有云存档。
  • 有广告或内购吗？都没有。本版本不含任何广告（连模拟广告画面都没有），也没有内购；所有道具都用游戏内金币或通关精华获取。
  • 关卡有时间限制吗？没有。任何关卡都没有倒计时、没有步数上限，也不会因超时失败，想多久就想多久。
  • 连胜怎么算？连续通关累积连胜。1 连胜开局自动倾倒 1 步，2 连胜自动 2 步，3 连胜起额外赠送 1 次免费撤回。
  • 能跟狐狸和小龙互动吗？可以。点药柜上的任意一只，它会用云朵气泡说一句自己的台词，几秒后自动消失。
  • 关卡感觉无解？每关都经求解器校验，不用道具也能过，放心用撤销与洗牌。
  • 没有声音？检查侧边静音拨片与音量，再进游戏设置确认音效与音乐都已开启。
  • 色弱能玩吗？设置里打开「色彩符号」，每种颜色会显示专属符号。
