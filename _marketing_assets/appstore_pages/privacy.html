Privacy Policy

Potion Sort: Alchemy · Last updated: November 25, 2026
Summary: Potion Sort: Alchemy does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your own device.
《药水排序》不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。你的游戏记录只保存在你自己的设备上。

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The game has no account system, no login, no sign-up, and no server that receives your data.

The following is stored locally on your device only (iOS local storage), and is deleted when you delete the app:

  • Level progress, highest level reached, cleared count
  • In-game coins, boosters, lives, streak records
  • Daily check-in records and achievement gallery unlocks
  • Audio, haptics, language, and accessibility preferences

2. Analytics, Advertising & Tracking

The current version contains no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking technology. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), we do not fingerprint your device, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The game therefore does not display an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Any advertisement-like screen shown inside the game is a locally rendered placeholder that never contacts an ad network.

3. Children's Privacy

The game is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). Because we do not collect any data at all, we do not knowingly collect information from children. There are no chat features, no user-generated content, and no external links aimed at children.

4. In-App Purchases

The game offers one optional non-consumable purchase ("Remove All Ads Forever"). Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your name, card number, or billing address — Apple only tells the app whether the purchase succeeded.

5. Network Access

The game runs fully offline. No gameplay data is uploaded. Deleting the app removes all locally stored progress permanently.

6. Your Rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on our side. To erase all game data, simply delete the app from your device (or use Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Potion Sort → Delete App).

7. Changes to This Policy

If a future version introduces online features, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
